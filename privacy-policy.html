Rise 365 by Groupe ABK

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

01Overview

Rise 365 ("we", "us") is developed and operated by Groupe ABK. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the app and how we use it.

02Information we collect

Your email address and profile details (username, display name, bio, avatar); the challenges you join and the daily tasks you complete; friend connections and activity you choose to share; and basic diagnostic information needed to keep the app running. Task proof photos you capture stay on your device only — they are never uploaded to our servers.

03How we use it

To run and secure your account, sync your challenge progress across your devices, show your activity to friends you've connected with, send you optional daily reminders, and respond to the support requests you send us.

04Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the infrastructure providers that host the app (such as Supabase for authentication and our database, and Apple for push notifications and Sign in with Apple), and only as needed to operate the service.

05Your choices

You can edit your username, bio and avatar at any time from the Profile tab. To request a full export or deletion of your account and data, contact us at the address below.

06Children's privacy

Rise 365 is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

07Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. We'll update the date above whenever we do.

08Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at contact@groupeabk.com.
